## When you may need a service matters lawyer in Lahore

Your job, your pay and your pension are protected by law, and so is the procedure an employer must follow before taking action against you. You may want to speak to a service matters lawyer in Lahore if:

- you have received a **show-cause notice**, charge sheet or statement of allegations;
- you have been **suspended**, **removed**, **dismissed** or **compulsorily retired**;
- a junior colleague has been **promoted** ahead of you, or your **seniority** has been fixed wrongly;
- your **pension**, gratuity, GP Fund or other retirement benefits have been delayed or refused;
- your **regularisation**, upgradation or pay fixation has not been done in line with the rules or policy;
- you have been **transferred** in breach of policy, or penalised for reasons unrelated to your work.

## How we handle service matters

1. **Establish your status.** Whether you are a civil servant, a government employee who is not a civil servant, or an employee of a statutory corporation determines the forum. This is the first question we answer.
2. **Read the rules that apply.** Service matters turn on specific laws and rules: the Punjab Civil Servants Act 1974, the Punjab Employees Efficiency, Discipline and Accountability (PEEDA) Act 2006, the Civil Servants Act 1973 for federal employees, recruitment rules, seniority lists and pension rules.
3. **Reply to the notice.** A careful reply to a show-cause notice or charge sheet can end proceedings early or build the record for an appeal. We draft replies that address each allegation and point out procedural defects.
4. **Departmental appeal or representation.** We prepare the appeal or representation to the competent authority within the time allowed, which is usually a prerequisite for going further.
5. **Tribunal or High Court.** If the departmental remedy fails, we file an appeal before the **Punjab Service Tribunal** or **Federal Service Tribunal**, or, for employees outside the tribunal's jurisdiction, a [writ petition in the Lahore High Court](/practice-areas/writ-petition-lahore-high-court/).

Some service disputes involve contractual claims for dues against bodies that are not government departments; those may be filed as [civil suits in Lahore](/practice-areas/civil-lawyer-lahore/). If you are facing an FIR connected with your job, our [criminal lawyers](/practice-areas/criminal-lawyer-lahore/) can advise on bail, and our guide to [pre-arrest bail](/guides/pre-arrest-bail-explained/) explains how protection from arrest works.

## Documents usually required

- your appointment letter, service book or record of service, and any promotion or transfer orders;
- the show-cause notice, charge sheet, inquiry report and the final order you wish to challenge;
- your reply and any departmental appeal or representation already filed, with proof of submission;
- relevant seniority lists, recruitment or promotion rules, and policy letters;
- for pension matters: retirement order, pension papers and correspondence with the accounts office;
- any FIR or court orders if criminal proceedings are connected.

## Typical process and timeline

| Stage | What happens | Usual time |
|---|---|---|
| Show-cause or charge sheet | Reply filed; inquiry may be held | Within the time stated in the notice |
| Final order | Competent authority passes order | After inquiry and personal hearing |
| Departmental appeal | Appeal or representation to the appellate authority | Usually within 30 days of the order |
| Waiting period | Authority decides, or the period allowed by law passes | Up to about 90 days |
| Service Tribunal | Appeal filed and heard | Within the limitation period after the departmental stage |
| Further appeal | Petition for leave to appeal to the Supreme Court | Within the time fixed by law |

Time limits differ between federal and provincial laws and between types of order, so please get advice as soon as you receive a notice.
