## When you may need a corporate lawyer in Lahore

Getting the legal structure right at the start of a business is far cheaper than fixing it later. You may want to speak to a corporate or company registration lawyer in Lahore if you are:

- starting a business and deciding between a **sole proprietorship**, **partnership** or **private limited company**;
- registering a company with the **Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan (SECP)**, including a single-member company;
- bringing in a new investor or partner and need a **shareholders' agreement** or **partnership deed**;
- entering into contracts with suppliers, distributors, landlords, employees or foreign parties;
- behind on **annual returns** or other filings and want to regularise the company's record;
- in a dispute with a co-founder, partner or customer about money, control of the business or performance of a contract.

## How we handle corporate and commercial matters

1. **Understand the business.** We ask how the business makes money, who owns what, and what you expect over the next few years. Legal documents should fit the business, not the other way round.
2. **Choose and set up the structure.** For a company, we assist with name reservation, the memorandum and articles of association, incorporation through the SECP portal, and the steps that follow: the National Tax Number, bank account opening, and registers that must be kept.
3. **Draft and review agreements.** We prepare contracts in clear language, with sensible clauses on payment, delivery, termination, confidentiality, liability and dispute resolution, and we flag risks in contracts drafted by the other side.
4. **Keep you compliant.** Companies must hold an annual general meeting, file annual returns and notify the SECP of changes in directors, shareholding or registered address. We can maintain a simple compliance calendar for you.
5. **Resolve disputes.** When a commercial relationship breaks down, we start with a legal notice and negotiation. If that fails, we represent you in civil suits, arbitration or company matters before the court.

## Documents usually required

For **company registration**:

- CNICs (or passports for foreign nationals) of all proposed directors and shareholders;
- proposed company names in order of preference and a short description of the business;
- the registered office address in Lahore, with proof such as a utility bill or tenancy agreement;
- details of the shareholding and paid-up capital;
- contact numbers and email addresses of directors, which the SECP system uses for verification.

For **contracts and disputes**:

- the signed agreement and any amendments, emails or WhatsApp messages about it;
- invoices, purchase orders, delivery notes and payment records;
- the company's memorandum and articles, and minutes of relevant board or shareholder meetings.

## Partnerships and family businesses

Many Lahore businesses are family partnerships that have never written anything down. Problems tend to arise on the death of a partner or when the next generation joins. A properly drafted partnership deed, or conversion into a private limited company with clear shareholding, can prevent years of litigation later. Where a partner has died, questions of the heirs' rights in the business overlap with succession law; our guide to [inheritance rights in Pakistan](/guides/inheritance-rights-in-pakistan/) gives a general overview.
