## When you may need a property lawyer in Lahore

Property is often a family's largest asset, and disputes about it can last for years if the first steps are taken badly. You may want to speak to a property or inheritance lawyer in Lahore if:

- a parent or relative has died and you need to establish who the **legal heirs** are and what each person's share is (**wirasat**);
- your share of inherited property is being withheld, or a **mutation (intiqal)** has been entered without your name;
- co-owners cannot agree on how to divide a house, shop or agricultural land and you want a **partition** (division by the court, or sale and division of the proceeds);
- someone has taken **possession** of your property without right, or a tenant will not leave;
- you are buying or selling and want the **title documents** (registry, fard, allotment letter, transfer letter) checked before money changes hands;
- you have an agreement to sell and the other side will not complete the sale, so you need a suit for **specific performance**;
- a gift deed (**hiba**), sale deed or power of attorney has been used to transfer property without proper consent.

## How we handle property and inheritance cases

Property cases are won or lost on documents and limitation periods, so we begin with the paper trail.

1. **Trace the title.** We read the chain of documents: registered deeds, revenue records (fard, jamabandi, mutations), society or authority files (for example LDA or a housing society), and any earlier court orders.
2. **Identify the heirs and shares.** In inheritance matters we prepare a family tree and work out each person's share under the applicable personal law, including the right of grandchildren of a predeceased son or daughter under Section 4 of the Muslim Family Laws Ordinance 1961.
3. **Choose the forum.** Depending on the facts, the case may belong in the Civil Court (declaration, partition, possession, specific performance, cancellation of documents), before a revenue officer (correction of mutation, partition of agricultural land), before the Sessions Court (illegal dispossession), or before the Ombudsperson for women's property rights.
4. **Protect the property early.** Where there is a risk of sale or construction, we can apply for a **temporary injunction** (stay order) so the property stays as it is while the case is decided. See our page on [civil suits and injunctions in Lahore](/practice-areas/civil-lawyer-lahore/).
5. **Negotiate where sensible.** Many family property disputes end in a written family settlement. We draft settlements so they can be registered or recorded in court and enforced later.

Where a property dispute arises from a divorce or a claim for haq mehr, we work alongside our [family lawyers in Lahore](/practice-areas/family-lawyer-lahore/). Decisions of revenue and housing authorities can sometimes be challenged through a [writ petition in the Lahore High Court](/practice-areas/writ-petition-lahore-high-court/).

## Documents usually required

Bring copies of whatever you have. We can usually obtain certified copies of public records on your behalf.

- CNICs of the deceased (if relevant) and of the legal heirs;
- the death certificate and **family registration certificate (FRC)** from NADRA;
- title documents: registered sale deed (registry), gift deed, transfer letter, allotment letter, or the society's membership file;
- revenue records: **fard-e-malkiat**, jamabandi and copies of relevant mutations;
- any agreement to sell, receipts for payments made, and correspondence with the other side;
- photographs of the property and any evidence of who is in possession and since when;
- earlier court orders, notices or police reports.

## Typical process and timeline

Contested property cases take longer than most other civil matters, and we will not promise you a fixed date. A common pattern for a suit for declaration and partition in Lahore is:

| Stage | What happens | Usual time |
|---|---|---|
| Review and notices | Title check, family tree, legal notice to the other side | 1–3 weeks |
| Filing | Plaint and application for temporary injunction filed in the Civil Court | Shortly after instructions |
| Interim relief | Court hears the stay application; status quo orders are often made early | First few hearings |
| Written statement and issues | Defendants reply; the court frames the disputed points (issues) | 2–6 months |
| Evidence | Witnesses and documents produced and cross-examined | Often the longest stage |
| Decree and execution | Preliminary and final decree; division by the court or sale | Depends on the property |

Applications to correct a mutation before a revenue officer, and complaints under the Illegal Dispossession Act 2005, generally move faster than a full civil suit.

## Inheritance shares and women's rights

Under Islamic law as applied in Pakistan, inheritance opens immediately on death. A son's share is generally twice a daughter's; a widow takes one-eighth where there are children and one-quarter where there are none; and parents also have fixed shares. The precise shares depend on who survives, which is why we prepare a family tree first. Our guide to [inheritance rights in Pakistan](/guides/inheritance-rights-in-pakistan/) explains the main shares and the steps to claim them in more detail.

Women are frequently pressured to give up their shares. In Punjab, the Enforcement of Women's Property Rights Act 2021 lets a woman complain to the Ombudsperson where she is kept out of her property, and Section 498-A of the Pakistan Penal Code makes it an offence to deprive a woman of her inheritance by deceitful or illegal means. A "relinquishment" signed under pressure can often be challenged.
