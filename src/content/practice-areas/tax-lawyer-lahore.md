## When you may need a tax lawyer in Lahore

Tax disputes often start with a single letter. You may want to speak to a tax lawyer in Lahore if you have received:

- a **notice** from an Inland Revenue officer asking you to explain income, assets, bank deposits, property purchases or foreign travel;
- a notice proposing to **amend an assessment**, or an order creating a demand you believe is wrong;
- a **show-cause notice** for sales tax or federal excise, or a notice from the **Punjab Revenue Authority (PRA)** about sales tax on services;
- notice of **recovery proceedings**, such as attachment of a bank account;
- a notice about **withholding tax** obligations as an employer or business.

You may also want advice before a large transaction, such as selling property, receiving a gift from abroad or restructuring a business, so that the tax treatment is understood in advance.

## How we handle tax matters

1. **Identify the legal basis.** Every notice is issued under a specific section of the Income Tax Ordinance 2001, the Sales Tax Act 1990, the Punjab Sales Tax on Services Act 2012 or related law. The section determines what the officer can ask for, the time limits and your rights.
2. **Check jurisdiction and limitation.** We confirm that the officer has authority over you and that the notice is within time. Notices issued without jurisdiction or after the limitation period can be challenged on that ground alone.
3. **Gather evidence with your accountant.** We work with you or your accountant to collect bank statements, sale and purchase records and explanations for each point raised.
4. **Reply in writing.** We prepare a clear, documented reply that answers what has been asked, without volunteering unnecessary information.
5. **Appeal where needed.** If an adverse order is passed, we file an appeal before the Commissioner Inland Revenue (Appeals) and, if necessary, the Appellate Tribunal Inland Revenue, with an application to stay recovery where appropriate.

Where a department acts without lawful authority and no adequate remedy is available under the tax statutes, a [writ petition in the Lahore High Court](/practice-areas/writ-petition-lahore-high-court/) may be possible. Companies with ongoing tax questions often combine this work with advice from our [corporate lawyers in Lahore](/practice-areas/corporate-lawyer-lahore/).

## Documents usually required

- the notice or order, including the envelope or email showing when you received it;
- your income tax returns and wealth statements for the years in question;
- bank statements, salary certificates, rent agreements or business accounts relevant to the points raised;
- sale and purchase records, sales tax returns and invoices for sales tax matters;
- documents for property or vehicles mentioned in the notice;
- earlier correspondence with the department and any previous orders.

## Typical process and timeline

| Stage | What happens | Usual time |
|---|---|---|
| Notice | Officer issues notice with a reply date | Reply period stated in the notice |
| Reply | Written reply and documents filed; hearing before the officer | Within the stated time (an extension can sometimes be requested) |
| Order | Officer passes an order, which may create a demand | After hearing |
| First appeal | Appeal to Commissioner Inland Revenue (Appeals) | Within 30 days of the order |
| Second appeal | Appeal to Appellate Tribunal Inland Revenue | Within 60 days of the appellate order |
| Reference | Reference to the High Court on a question of law | Within 90 days of the Tribunal's order |

The time limits above are those generally applicable under the Income Tax Ordinance 2001. Sales tax and provincial tax matters have their own deadlines, so always check the specific law with an advocate.

## Tax on inherited property and gifts

People who inherit property or receive gifts from relatives are sometimes surprised to receive questions from the FBR about the source of an asset. Inheritance and certain gifts are generally not taxed as income, but the transfer should be properly documented and declared in the wealth statement. Our guide to [inheritance rights in Pakistan](/guides/inheritance-rights-in-pakistan/) explains how inherited property is transferred, which helps when you need to show where an asset came from.
