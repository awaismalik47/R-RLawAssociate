Rana Adeel Raza is an Advocate of the Lahore High Court and an associate at R&R Law Associates. He has been in practice for five years and appears before the Lahore High Court and the district and sessions courts in Lahore.

His work covers civil and criminal litigation, including bail and pre-arrest bail matters, family cases such as khula, custody and maintenance, and property and inheritance disputes. He is closely involved in case preparation, from drafting petitions and written statements to managing court hearings, and keeps clients updated on each stage of their matter.

He completed his LL.B. at the University of the Punjab in 2021 and is a Life Member of both the Lahore High Court Bar Association and the Lahore Bar Association. He advises clients in English, Urdu and Punjabi.
