Ali Raza Bajwa is an Advocate of the Lahore High Court and a founding partner of R&R Law Associates. With 12 years of practice, he regularly appears before the Lahore High Court and the district and sessions courts in Lahore.

His practice covers civil and criminal litigation, including bail and pre-arrest bail matters, constitutional writ petitions, family cases such as khula, custody and maintenance, and property and inheritance disputes. He works closely with each client from the first consultation, explaining the legal position, the likely process and the documents required in plain terms, and keeps clients informed at every stage of their case.
